---

## Product

CFMI is an **AI dig tool**. Point it at a bill or topic; it produces an educational **Civic Action Pack**: diagnosis, Charter score, both sides, influence leads from public records, open fix language, a sample legislator letter, and a social blurb—under published methods, with a human editor before publish.

## Mission

CFMI digs so people can (1) understand issues, (2) find constitutional, free-market, people-over-bureaucracy solutions, and (3) expose corruption or manipulation of the system. Free markets are a primary instrument against artificial barriers and cronyism. AI is a disclosed research aid—never an authority. Charter principles bind every dig.

## Problem

Opaque bureaucracy and privileged interests hide in bill text and topic narratives. Messaging alone does not return power to accountable people under clear rules—or surface where proposed language fails Charter filters on constitutional limits, free markets, and anti-capture.

## Method

| Lane | What donors get |
|------|-----------------|
| **Dig** | Research-funded AI investigation of bills and topics under Charter filters |
| **Understand & score** | Public diagnosis and Hard Flags where text concentrates power in opaque bureaucracy or privileged interests |
| **Both sides & influence** | Steelmanned cases plus disclosed influence trails from public filings |
| **Fix & act** | Open fix language + educational letter/social templates (Civic Action Pack) |
| **Bind** | Charter principles on every dig, draft, and comment cycle |

Charter filters always bind. Open comment never means adding subsidies, barriers, or opaque discretion to buy support.
