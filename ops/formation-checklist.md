# Formation Checklist — CFMI

**Status:** Bootstrap planning artifact only.  
**Default path:** **501(c)(3)** educational and research organization.  
**Attorney-review required** at every legal step below.  
**Do not claim tax-exempt status** until counsel confirms an IRS determination (or other applicable recognition) is in hand.

This checklist is not legal advice. Form entity type, activities, and filings with qualified counsel.

---

## 0. Why (c)(3) is the default

CFMI’s public model is **education and research**:

- Score legislation and publish conflicts with Charter goals  
- Provide open fix / alignment language  
- Circulate sample acts for bipartisan review and comment  
- Disclose AI methods and rubrics  

That framing fits a **501(c)(3)** educational/research nonprofit better than a primary lobbying vehicle—**subject to attorney confirmation**.

### Future (c)(4) note

If CFMI later shifts into **heavy vote-lobbying** (substantial attempts to influence specific legislation as a core program, beyond educational commentary and research), counsel may advise a **501(c)(4)** social-welfare entity, a dual structure ((c)(3) + (c)(4)), or other arrangements. **Do not self-convert or claim either status without attorney review.** Track this as a future decision, not a current claim.

## 5. Activity guardrails while in formation

Allowed under the educational/research default (**confirm with counsel**):

- Publishing scores, conflict analyses, methodology, and sample acts for open comment  
- Disclosing AI rubrics and anti-capture rules  
- Seeking aligned support for research infrastructure and formation  

Escalate to counsel before:

- Substantial grassroots or direct lobbying campaigns aimed at specific votes  
- Explicit “call your member to vote X” programs as a primary activity  
- PAC-like activity or candidate support  

Heavy vote-lobbying → revisit **(c)(4) or dual structure** with counsel.

---

## 6. Manual-only founder steps

1. Retain nonprofit counsel (and CPA if advised).  
2. Confirm (c)(3) default vs (c)(4) given planned activities.  
3. File formation documents as counsel directs.  
4. Obtain EIN; open bank account.  
5. File exemption application only when counsel says the package is ready.  
6. Update site/pitch to reflect **actual** status—never aspirational status as fact.
