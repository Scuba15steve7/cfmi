## Summary (≤ 120 words)

The Fiscal Responsibility Act pairs debt-limit and spending provisions with **Title III permitting reforms** (including the Builder Act amendments to NEPA practice) and a **project-specific expediting mandate for the Mountain Valley Pipeline**. Generally applicable page/time limits and lead-agency clarity can lower opaque process barriers—Charter-aligned. The named-project fast lane is a **legal privilege** unavailable on equal terms, a Hard Flag even inside an otherwise mixed permitting title.

## Conflicts with Charter goals

1. **Named-project privilege** for Mountain Valley Pipeline expediting—not a generally applicable rule.  
2. **Artificial manipulation** — legal fast lane unavailable to similarly situated competitors on equal published terms.  
3. **Incomplete transparency** — permitting gains are real but still leave substantial agency judgment unless further narrowed.  
4. **Precedent risk** — future packages may trade debt votes for project earmarks.

## Hard Flags

- [x] Dimension ≤ 1 (F)
- [x] Artificial manipulation match (legal privilege / named project)

1. **Mountain Valley Pipeline expediting** provisions (P.L. 118-5, Title III) — project-specific mandate.  
2. **Monitoring flag:** residual NEPA discretion where Builder Act leaves multi-factor judgment without fully objective completeness checklists.

## Proposed alignment changes (open fix language for bipartisan comment)

1. **Strike** project-specific expediting; replace with **generally applicable** shot clocks, page limits, and lead-agency rules for **all** similarly situated energy infrastructure applications.  
2. **Define** “complete application” with a published checklist; **deem** failure-to-act as approval or denial on a fixed clock (choose one; publish).  
3. **Require** machine-readable docketing of all NEPA timelines and waiver requests.  
4. **Separate** debt-limit legislation from **any** named-project titles in future vehicles.

## Least-Coercive Alternative Sketch

Keep generally applicable Builder Act–style process limits; omit earmarked pipelines or plants; let equal rules clear or deny all applicants.
