## Summary (≤ 120 words)

The Inflation Reduction Act layers large **energy and manufacturing tax credits**, grant and loan programs, and domestic-content / prevailing-wage adders onto selected clean-energy and industrial activities. Separate health and tax-administration titles are outside CFMI’s core market-barrier focus. The energy-industrial core steers capital through political eligibility stacks (technology lists, content rules, agency guidance), creating durable rents and raising rivals’ costs for non-favored paths—while leaving many permitting barriers untouched.

## Conflicts with Charter goals

1. **Political allocation of capital** via stacked energy and manufacturing credits.  
2. **Artificial manipulation** — domestic-content and wage adders function as transferable privileges contingent on compliance with politically set checklists.  
3. **Opaque discretion** — much economic effect lives in guidance and “qualified facility” determinations.  
4. **Ignores least-coercive path** — does not primarily clear generally applicable barriers to building energy infrastructure.

## Hard Flags

- [x] Dimension ≤ 1 (D, E, F, G)
- [x] Artificial manipulation match (subsidy / privilege)

1. **Clean electricity / investment / production credit architecture** (IRA energy tax titles) with technology and bonus eligibility gates.  
2. **Advanced manufacturing / industrial credit and loan-adjacent programs** steering capital to favored projects.  
3. **Domestic content and labor adders** unavailable on equal terms to otherwise identical projects.

## Proposed alignment changes (open fix language for bipartisan comment)

1. **Sunset** technology-specific bonus rates; replace with a **single, declining, technology-neutral** capital-cost recovery schedule—or repeal sector credits and reduce generally applicable rates.  
2. **Strike** domestic-content and prevailing-wage **bonus** multipliers that privilege politically compliant projects; keep any labor/safety rules of general application outside the tax code.  
3. **Publish** objective, machine-readable eligibility tests; bar secret letter rulings as the practical path to credits.  
4. **Pair any remaining transition credits** with **generally applicable** permitting shot clocks—not with new grant competitions.

## Least-Coercive Alternative Sketch

Neutral tax treatment + open entry/permitting for energy projects; no stacked industrial privileges.
