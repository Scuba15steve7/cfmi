## CFMI response

How we weigh both sides against [CHARTER.md](../../CHARTER.md) filters:

1. **What we accept** — Legitimate safety / asymmetric-information concerns that survive scrutiny.  
2. **What we reject or narrow** — Barriers, rents, capture, or opaque discretion that fail least-coercive and open-entry tests.  
3. **Least-coercive path** — Rights clarification, certification, bonding, targeted safety rules, sunsets, objective criteria—before full licensure or new bureaucracy.  
4. **Bipartisan comment** — Sample or fix language published for cross-aisle review; Charter hard limits still bind.
