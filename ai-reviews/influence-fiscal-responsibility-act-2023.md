## 1. Hard-Flagged provisions (from parent review)

1. **Mountain Valley Pipeline expediting** (P.L. 118-5, Title III) — project-specific mandate / legal privilege.  
2. **Monitoring flag:** residual NEPA discretion where Builder Act–style reforms leave multi-factor judgment without fully objective completeness checklists.

## 3. What we do not allege

- Bribery, illegal coordination, or secret deals beyond statute + public filings.  
- That generally applicable NEPA reforms are a privilege for MVP (they are scored separately and more favorably).  
- Personal attacks on named legislators; record their public positions and FEC/OpenSecrets data when cited.

## 4. Open fix / alignment alternatives

→ [Proposed alignment changes — FRA review](./fiscal-responsibility-act-2023.md#proposed-alignment-changes-open-fix-language-for-bipartisan-comment)

In short: strike project-specific expediting; keep generally applicable shot clocks/page limits/lead-agency rules for all similarly situated projects; define complete applications with published checklists; separate debt-limit bills from named-project titles.
