## 1. Hard-Flagged provisions (from parent review)

1. **Competitive grant programs** with open-ended merit factors and secretarial priority lists.  
2. **Buy America / domestic content preferences** that privilege certain suppliers.  
3. **Program-specific set-asides** not available on equal published terms to all otherwise eligible applicants.

## 3. What we do not allege

- That unions or contractors designed Buy America expansions or secretarial priority lists.  
- That support for “infrastructure jobs” equals support for opaque competitive allocation.  
- Causation from any PAC or lobbying dollar to a specific grant program.

---

## 4. Open fix / alignment alternatives

→ [Proposed alignment changes — IIJA review](./infrastructure-investment-jobs-act.md#proposed-alignment-changes-open-fix-language-for-bipartisan-comment)

In short: shift beauty-contest grants to objective formulas; narrow Buy America with published waiver findings or equal trade/tax treatment; require machine-readable award justifications; add generally applicable permitting shot clocks without ideology-based fast lanes.
