# Public suggestions

Public intake for bills, regulations, and topics people want CFMI to look at. Suggestions arrive as GitHub Issues (template: [suggest-review.yml](../../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/suggest-review.yml)). AI screens and ranks them against the Charter; a human editor publishes status in the queue.

Interest disclosure is mandatory. Educational research only—not legal advice or voting instructions. Bipartisan welcome under the same Charter filters.
