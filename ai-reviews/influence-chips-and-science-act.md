## 1. Hard-Flagged provisions (from parent review)

1. **Subsidy / rent:** ~$39B fabrication incentives and loan/guarantee authority (CHIPS incentives / NDAA §9902 framework).  
2. **Tax privilege:** Advanced manufacturing investment credit for semiconductor manufacturing property (25% structure as enacted).  
3. **Discretionary selection:** Commerce designation of funded projects and negotiated agreement terms.

## 4. Open fix / alignment alternatives

Charter analysis and amendment sketches remain in the parent review:

→ [Proposed alignment changes — CHIPS review](./chips-and-science-act.md#proposed-alignment-changes-open-fix-language-for-bipartisan-comment)

In short: wind down fab subsidy/loan authority in favor of open competitive procurement where national security requires chips; convert or repeal sector ITC toward equal capital-cost recovery or lower general rates; publish award criteria; prefer equal permitting shot clocks without sector grants.
