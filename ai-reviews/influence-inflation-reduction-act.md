## 1. Hard-Flagged provisions (from parent review)

1. **Clean electricity / investment / production credit architecture** with technology and bonus eligibility gates.  
2. **Advanced manufacturing / industrial credit and loan-adjacent programs** steering capital to favored projects.  
3. **Domestic content and labor adders** unavailable on equal terms to otherwise identical projects.

## 4. Open fix / alignment alternatives

→ [Proposed alignment changes — IRA review](./inflation-reduction-act.md#proposed-alignment-changes-open-fix-language-for-bipartisan-comment)

In short: sunset technology-specific bonuses; prefer technology-neutral recovery or lower general rates; strike domestic-content/wage **bonus** multipliers; publish objective eligibility tests; pair any transition credits with generally applicable permitting clocks—not new grant beauty contests.
