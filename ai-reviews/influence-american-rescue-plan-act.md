## 1. Hard-Flagged provisions (from parent review)

1. **State and Local Fiscal Recovery Funds** with broad eligible-use categories refined through Treasury guidance.  
2. **Sector and program expansions** allocated by political design rather than equal, published claims.  
3. **Weak sunsets / clawback clarity** relative to outlay scale (pattern concern for future vehicles).

## 3. What we do not allege

- That governors or municipal leagues intended industrial subsidies under emergency labels.  
- That any healthcare or restaurant lobbyist wrote the SLFRF statute.  
- Criminal or corrupt intent from disclosed lobbying or public aid requests.

---

## 4. Open fix / alignment alternatives

ARPA is enacted; parent review offers **templates for future emergency vehicles**:

→ [Proposed alignment changes — ARPA review](./american-rescue-plan-act.md#proposed-alignment-changes-open-fix-language-for-bipartisan-comment)

In short: exhaustive eligible-use lists with numerical triggers; hard sunsets and machine-readable reporting; bar industrial subsidies and named corporate grants inside relief titles; equal treatment without sector carve-outs unless tied to objective closure orders.
