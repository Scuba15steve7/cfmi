Bill reviews

Living and recent major federal packages scored against the Charter. We publish conflicts, Hard Flags with language hooks, open alignment language for bipartisan comment—and, where public filings allow, disclosed influence trails alongside those scores. Not vote whips.

Charter-aligned scores

Rubric dimensions A–G (0–5; higher = more aligned). Any dimension ≤ 1 or any artificial-manipulation match is a Hard Flag. Full reviews, fix language, influence companions (LDA / OpenSecrets–style facts only), and disclosures live in the repository. Educational research only—see Methodology §7.

  • CHIPS and Science Act

    P.L. 117-167 · composite 1.6

    Semiconductor fab grants, loan authority, and a manufacturing investment credit allocate capital by political selection. National-security framing does not erase the rent surface.

    Alignment sketch Prefer open procurement and equal permitting over sector subsidies; publish award criteria or wind down incentive authority.

    Full review in repo · Influence notes

  • Inflation Reduction Act

    P.L. 117-169 · composite 1.4

    Stacked energy and industrial tax credits with domestic-content and labor adders steer investment through eligibility gates and agency guidance.

    Alignment sketch Sunset technology-specific bonuses; favor technology-neutral recovery or lower general rates; publish objective eligibility tests.

    Full review in repo · Influence notes

  • Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act

    P.L. 117-58 · composite 2.0

    Core formula funding can be rules-based; competitive grant pots, Buy America preferences, and multi-factor “merit” scoring add opaque political allocation.

    Alignment sketch Shift beauty-contest grants to objective formulas; narrow preferences; require machine-readable award justifications and equal permitting clocks.

    Full review in repo · Influence notes

  • American Rescue Plan Act

    P.L. 117-2 · composite 1.7

    Large COVID-era fiscal transfers, including state and local recovery funds with broad eligible uses, created a lasting discretion and rent surface under emergency labels.

    Alignment sketch For future emergency vehicles: exhaustive use lists, hard sunsets, public reporting, and a bar on industrial subsidies inside relief titles.

    Full review in repo · Influence notes

  • Fiscal Responsibility Act of 2023

    P.L. 118-5 · composite 2.4

    Builder Act–style NEPA process limits can reduce opacity for everyone; the Mountain Valley Pipeline expediting mandate is a named legal privilege—a Hard Flag.

    Alignment sketch Keep generally applicable shot clocks; strike project-specific fast lanes; define complete applications with published checklists.

    Full review in repo · Influence notes

Influence & transparency
