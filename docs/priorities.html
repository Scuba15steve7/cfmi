How we choose

We start with what the public says are the biggest national issues—then keep only what fits the Charter. Polling describes concern, not truth.

Two filters, in order

  1. Public salience. Recent national polls (Gallup, Pew, AP-NORC, CBS/YouGov, and related) show what large shares of Americans name as top problems: healthcare costs, inflation and cost of living, government dysfunction and money in politics, immigration, the deficit, housing, and others.
  2. Charter fit. We score each public label for people-over-bureaucracy, free-market barriers, government overreach, cronyism, constitutional process, and whether CFMI’s AI products can help. Poll slogans are remapped to Charter mechanisms first (e.g. “crime” → access to justice / justice bureaucracy). Free markets matter; the deeper through-line is returning power from opaque bureaucracy to the people. High salience with weak Charter fit does not become a CFMI lead.

Full sources, scores, and the salience × fit matrix are in the repository research note. Methods sit in Methodology §1.1.

CFMI priority targets

Ranked by public salience × Charter fit (August 2026 working agenda). Each target names the main legislative or regulatory roadblock and what CFMI publishes.

  1. Healthcare supply barriers

    Public: healthcare costs · Score 25

    Roadblock: State Certificate of Need statutes and practice-act scope limits that ration facilities and clinicians.

    CFMI product: Score CON and scope bills; open least-coercive fix language; sample acts for bipartisan comment.

    Certificate of Need · Licensing · Accreditation

  2. Housing zoning & permitting

    Public: housing, cost of living · Score 20

    Roadblock: Local exclusionary zoning and discretionary review (delay, soft vetoes, fee stacks); environmental-review regimes used as entry barriers where they apply.

    CFMI product: Score housing bills for buried barriers; by-right and shot-clock sample language.

    Housing brief · Examples

  3. Occupational licensing

    Public: economy, wages, access to care · Score 15–25 (cross-cutting)

    Roadblock: State licensing statutes and board rules that raise rivals’ costs without clear harm tests.

    CFMI product: Score expansions; sunrise-review and certification-first alternatives.

    Licensing brief

  4. Industrial subsidies & privileges

    Public: deficit, fairness, economy · Score 16

    Roadblock: Statutory industrial-policy packages—firm- or sector-specific subsidies and tax privileges (e.g. CHIPS and Science Act; Inflation Reduction Act credit design).

    CFMI product: Score living subsidy bills; open language that removes rents; disclosed influence companions from public filings.

    Subsidies brief · Bill reviews

  5. Opaque discretionary permitting

    Public: government dysfunction, housing, energy · Score 20 (shared)

    Roadblock: Process statutes and agency waiver authority without published objective criteria (NEPA-class stacks and state analogs).

    CFMI product: Score permitting bills; fix language for criteria, clocks, and publication.

    Permitting brief

  6. Access to justice & anti-corruption in public safety / courts

    Public: crime *(remapped)*, corruption, government dysfunction · Score 15

    Roadblock: Civil asset forfeiture and equitable sharing (agency-keep); state UPL / practice acts blocking affordable legal help; court fee/fine revenue systems; opaque charging and discovery; public-defender undercapacity; QI and bail bills that need mechanism scoring—not slogans.

    CFMI product: Score justice and public-safety process bills; publish rent, barrier, and opacity conflicts; open least-coercive transparency language. Frame: people over justice bureaucracy—free markets where rents and entry barriers apply; not tough-on-crime or defund theater.

    Access to justice brief

  7. Election administration transparency

    Public: government, democracy, trust · Score 15

    Roadblock: NVRA attestation-without-verification gaps and opaque roll/audit practice; living bills such as the SAVE Act that mix verification with design tradeoffs.

    CFMI product: Score living election bills; federal transparency package and model outline—auditable rules over opaque admin practice, not slogans.

    Election brief · Transparency package

  8. Professional accreditation cartels

    Public: healthcare, education · Score 12

    Roadblock: Accreditation and related capacity gates (including Title IV–linked structures) that ration professional entry.

    CFMI product: Score bills that harden cartel gates; least-coercive alternatives for comment.

    Accreditation brief

What we do not lead on

Some poll labels score weakly until remapped. Raw crime theater (tough-on-crime or defund) is not a CFMI lead; we remap it to access to justice and anti-corruption in courts and public-safety bureaucracy—people over bureaucracy, with free markets where rents and entry barriers apply. Immigration remains high-salience / weak Charter product fit. Macro inflation matters to the public; we address it through supply-side barriers and privileges that raise prices, not as a Federal Reserve commentary project.

Regional or emerging topics (western water transfers, closed AI market rules) stay in the repository as method samples until salience and fit justify a higher slot.
