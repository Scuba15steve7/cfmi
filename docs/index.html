Mission

CFMI is an AI tool that digs into legislation and public topics so people can (1) understand the issues, (2) find constitutional, free-market, people-over-bureaucracy solutions, and (3) expose corruption or manipulation of the system. Free markets are a primary instrument against artificial barriers and cronyism; Charter principles bind every dig. We publish educational Civic Action Packs—diagnosis, Charter scores, both sides, influence leads from public records, fix language, sample legislator letters, and social blurbs—under disclosed AI methods. AI is a research aid under published rules—never an authority.

How we work

Dig into living bills and topics. Publish diagnosis and conflicts. Steelman both sides. Map public-record influence. Offer open fix language and educational action templates—always under Charter filters.

  1. Dig Use research-funded, rules-bound AI to investigate bills and topics under Charter filters.
  2. Understand & score Publish diagnosis and Hard Flags where text concentrates power in opaque bureaucracy or privileged interests—or conflicts with constitutional limits, free markets, or anti-capture filters.
  3. Both sides & influence Steelman reform and status-quo cases; where public filings allow, disclosed influence trails beside the score.
  4. Fix & act Offer open fix language plus educational legislator-letter and social templates (Civic Action Pack).
  5. Bind Charter principles govern every dig, draft, and comment cycle.

Examples

Licensing cartels, housing bans, Certificate of Need, frozen water transfers, opaque permits—places where government, not open competition, holds up progress. Short issue briefs show what CFMI’s AI dig is built to find. Agenda order comes from public salience filtered by the Charter.

Principles

Non-negotiable filters for every dig and draft. Full text in the Charter.

  • Constitutional fidelity — enumerated powers, federalism, separation of powers, property and contract.
  • Free markets — prices, voluntary exchange, and open entry as a primary check on artificial barriers and cronyism.
  • No artificial manipulation — no incumbent subsidies, entry barriers, opaque discretion, or insulating privileges.
  • Minimal intervention — least coercive instrument first.
  • Radical transparency — methods, AI use, funding, and disclosed influence trails public by default.
  • Anti-capture — we must never become a source of new rents.
  • Competitive federalism — never federal commandeering.
