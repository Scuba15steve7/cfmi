Suggest a review

Point CFMI at a bill, regulation, or topic. Suggestions are public. AI screens and ranks them against Charter values—not party lines. Interest disclosure is required.

How it works

Zero paid intake. GitHub Issues keep suggestions versioned and visible—radical transparency by default.

  1. Suggest Open a public issue with the review template: what to look at, why it matters, jurisdiction, and mandatory interest disclosure.
  2. AI screens & ranks Disclosed, rules-bound AI scores fit against Charter principles and Methodology problem-ranking weights. A human editor accepts or revises before anything is published as ranked or declined.
  3. Public queue Status lands in the open queue—Pending, Ranked, or Declined with a reason. No private side channel for rents or pay-for-play.

Ground rules

  • Interest disclosure is mandatory. Employment, clients, subsidies, or other material interests related to the subject in the prior 24 months—or write “None.”
  • Charter filters, not caucus filters. Suggestions from any party or none are welcome under the same rules.
  • Anti-capture. Asks for subsidies, entry barriers, opaque discretion, or named privileges are declined and logged.
  • Educational research. Not legal advice, voting instructions, or counsel to any person. Ranking is not a promise of a full review on a deadline.
