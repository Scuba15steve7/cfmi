About CFMI

An AI tool that digs into legislation and topics—helping people understand issues, find constitutional and free-market solutions that put people over bureaucracy, and expose corruption or system manipulation.

Mission

CFMI is an AI tool that digs into legislation and public topics so people can (1) understand the issues, (2) find constitutional, free-market, people-over-bureaucracy solutions, and (3) expose corruption or manipulation of the system. Free markets are a primary instrument against artificial barriers and cronyism; Charter principles bind every dig. Where public records allow, we publish disclosed influence trails alongside scores—radical transparency, not private investigation. We publish educational Civic Action Packs and sample acts for bipartisan comment. AI is a research aid under published rules—never an authority.

How we investigate

Deeper issue and bill work uses hierarchical AI investigation under public rubrics: an orchestrator frames the problem, specialist lanes cover text, root cause, disclosed influence, counters, safeguards, and fix language, and a human editor accepts or revises before anything is published. Methods stay in the open repository—not a black box. See the public Tool page.

Bootstrap story

CFMI began as a one-founder, zero-budget project. An AI dig tool aimed at understanding issues, finding people-over-bureaucracy solutions, and exposing system manipulation should show up first as a clear product, a public charter, and an online presence—before a large budget. Work is advanced in the open. Anti-capture funding rules are written before money arrives. Formation planning defaults to a 501(c)(3) educational/research model with attorney review—we do not claim tax status.

What aligned support means

Aligned support helps CFMI:

  • Keep the Tool live and archived
  • Formalize governance and pursue appropriate tax recognition with counsel (default: 501(c)(3) educational/research)
  • Fund research infrastructure for digs and Civic Action Packs
  • Circulate sample acts for bipartisan review and comment under public rubrics

It does not mean purchasing outcomes, scores, silence, subsidies, barriers, or privileges for a private interest. Those asks are refused under the Charter.

What we are not

  • Not a political party or PAC
  • Not a vehicle for incumbent protection
  • Not a black-box “AI lobby”
  • Not a federal commandeering project
  • Not claiming IRS tax-exempt status until counsel confirms recognition
