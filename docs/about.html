About CFMI

Using AI to score legislation, publish conflicts with free markets and constitutional limits, and offer open bipartisan fix language and sample bills.

Mission

CFMI is research-funded AI for legislative analysis: we score current bills against free markets and constitutional limits, publish conflicts with our Charter goals, and provide open alignment and fix language for proposed legislation. We publish sample acts for bipartisan review and comment, work to minimize unintended consequences, and bind every product to Charter principles. AI is a disclosed research aid under published rules—never an authority.

Bootstrap story

CFMI began as a one-founder, zero-budget project. Research-funded AI aimed at scoring bills, publishing conflicts with free markets and constitutional limits, and offering open bipartisan fix language should show up first as a clear mission, a public charter, and an online presence—before a large budget. Work is advanced in the open. Anti-capture funding rules are written before money arrives. Formation planning defaults to a 501(c)(3) educational/research model with attorney review—we do not claim tax status.

What aligned support means

Aligned support helps CFMI:

  • Keep this site live and archived
  • Formalize governance and pursue appropriate tax recognition with counsel (default: 501(c)(3) educational/research)
  • Fund research infrastructure for scoring and open fix language
  • Circulate sample acts for bipartisan review and comment under public rubrics

It does not mean purchasing outcomes, scores, silence, subsidies, barriers, or privileges for a private interest. Those asks are refused under the Charter.

What we are not

  • Not a political party or PAC
  • Not a vehicle for incumbent protection
  • Not a black-box “AI lobby”
  • Not a federal commandeering project
  • Not claiming IRS tax-exempt status until counsel confirms recognition
